# About

Watchwright is a browser test harness on [Playwright](https://playwright.dev). It runs long test and benchmark runs on your own machine, streams them live to its own web page, and lets you or your AI agents start, pause, fix and score them. It is free and open source under the [Apache-2.0 license](https://github.com/DumiFlex/watchwright/blob/main/LICENSE).

It is built and maintained by [DumiFlex](https://github.com/DumiFlex), an independent developer who also makes [Wildcard Pipeline](https://github.com/DumiFlex/ComfyUI-Wildcard-Pipeline), a ComfyUI extension for random prompts that make sense together. Wildcard Pipeline's AI bench was Watchwright's first test pack.

## Support the project
